✦ QUOTECRAFT STUDIO · PHOTO ESTIMATOR · QUICKQUOTE v2.2.0

📸 Picture it. Plan it. Quote it.

Take a site photo, add a known reference length or actual dimensions, and create a materials takeoff you can review before quoting.

Professional measurement note: Photos cannot reveal reliable feet/metres on their own. Mark a known-length reference on the same flat plane as the measurements. Perspective, angles, curves, grade, openings, underground services and code requirements must be verified on-site. This is a preliminary takeoff, not an engineered measurement.
📷 Choose a photo to mark measurements. You can also type actual dimensions below without a photo.
📏 How calibration works: Choose “Reference”, click both ends of something you measured with a tape (for example a 4-ft board), enter its real length below. Then choose Width and Height and mark their endpoints. Image measurements are approximate and only suitable for the same plane and depth as the reference.
